# AquaLink
### Water Closer to Home

**We left home to build a better future. We never forgot who we left behind.**

When we left our homes to pursue education, we carried more than our own ambitions. We carried the hopes, sacrifices and expectations of the people who raised us.

Many of those people never had the opportunities we have today. They sacrificed their comfort so that we could pursue a better future.

But while we were learning, life back home continued.

For many rural families, accessing something as basic as water remains a daily struggle. Elderly people, children and households still face the burden of unreliable water sources and long journeys to collect water.

As students pursuing careers in information technology, we believed our education should mean something beyond obtaining a qualification.

**AquaLink was born from our determination to use what we were learning to serve the communities that made our education possible.**

AquaLink is a proposed smart community water distribution and management solution that combines local water infrastructure with digital tools for managing households, suppliers, subscriptions and water usage.

This portfolio tells the story of that project, the team behind it, and my personal journey as Project Lead and Frontend Developer.
